## Must be bought or verified

### 1. Parker-compatible fountain-pen converter

The Parker IM's included blue refill is probably an ink cartridge. A converter is required to draw ink from the Hongdian bottle.

- First check the pen box in case a converter was included.
- If it was not, buy a **Parker Deluxe Fountain Pen Converter** or another converter explicitly compatible with the Parker IM.
- Do not assume a generic international converter will fit.

### 2. Fountain-pen-friendly writing paper

Recommended:

- **Clairefontaine Triomphe blank writing pad**
- Approximately A5 / 5.75 × 8.25 inches
- Cream or ivory preferred; white is acceptable if cream is unavailable

The paper should resist feathering and bleeding when used with fountain-pen ink.

### 3. Matching envelopes

Recommended:

- C6 envelopes in cream or ivory
- Fountain-pen-friendly surface
- Sized to accept the selected paper folded once

Confirm the paper's folded dimensions before ordering the envelopes.

### 4. Wax-melting spoon

- Small brass-colored spoon
- Preferably with a wooden handle
- Large enough for approximately 3–4 wax beads

### 5. Wax warmer and heat source

- Small wax-seal warmer that holds the melting spoon
- Unscented tealight candles
- A dark wood, black, or brass-colored warmer best matches the intended aesthetic

## Basic use

1. Install the converter in the Parker IM and fill it with bluish-gray ink.
2. Test the pen and ink on a spare sheet before writing the final letter.
3. Place a lined guide beneath the blank page if needed.
4. Write the letter and allow the ink to dry fully before folding it.
5. Seal the envelope flap normally.
6. Melt approximately 3–4 burgundy wax beads in the spoon.
7. Pour the wax into a compact circle.
8. Press the stamp into the wax and hold it still for roughly 10–15 seconds.
9. Let the wax cool completely before handling or mailing the envelope.

For mailed correspondence, the safest option is to make the wax impression separately on a silicone mat and attach it using a strong adhesive seal dot. Postal sorting equipment can damage a seal applied directly to the outside of an envelope. If applying it directly, use the flexible Nostalgic Impressions wax.
